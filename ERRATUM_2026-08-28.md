# Erratum · 28 August 2026

This erratum is published because the defects below are real and were found internally. It states
what was wrong and what changed. **It deliberately does not describe how to exploit anything, and
the proof artefacts that demonstrate the R+4 defects are withheld pending external review.**

---

## 1 · Signature-suite binding — R+2 reference suite and verifier

An adversarial-fuzz oracle in the R+2 reference suite was found unable to report the attack family
it enumerated: a verifier accepted a receipt whose **unsigned** algorithm label contradicted its
**signed** content, and the oracle's slip condition could never become true for that case.

Both the oracle and the **two** affected verifier surfaces were repaired on 28 August 2026, in
commits **`85e3d09`** and **`c66e25d`**. Both are required: `85e3d09` repairs the first verifier
surface and the oracle; `c66e25d` repairs the second surface and the test that had locked it. A
reference to `85e3d09` alone describes an incompletely repaired state.

**Corrected figures.** The suite runs **404,000 adversarial cases** (signature malleability ·
downgrade · Merkle forgery). Re-run with the corrected oracle against the frozen `audit-tob-v1.1.0`
code, it reports **309 slips out of 404,000**, not 0. Against the repaired implementation it reports
**0 slips**. The repair is **not** present in the published `audit-tob-v1.1.0` tag. Corrected
figures and the repaired-implementation commit hashes are published with the evidence set.

---

## 2 · The R+4 circuit

Defects were found in the original R+4 circuit — the one this repository publishes, and the one the
Base mainnet verifier covers — during internal review. They were repaired in a **new circuit**.

The new circuit is **not published**. It has **no trusted setup**, and therefore no ceremony, no
verifying key, and no deployed verifier. Until a fresh ceremony is run — with independent external
contributors, a **pre-announced** beacon height, a genuine ≥14-day contribution window, and dated
per-contributor attestations — there is nothing to publish and nothing to verify against.

**What is stated here, and nothing more:** the published circuit is
`reference/circuits/threshold-count.circom`, SHA-256
`3e4db0cb6ff5d32af706024327824cad711133ea0b0d2b22a887eeb67d9a8c9d`. It has never been modified
since it was first committed (`c398607`), and this erratum does not modify it either.

**Deliberately withheld:** the defect mechanisms, the constraint-level detail, the repaired
circuit's internals, and the proof artefacts that demonstrate the defects. They are not in this
repository and will not be added. This is a disclosure of *what*, not a recipe for *how*.

---

## 3 · What this erratum does not do

- It does not edit or delete anything already published. The circuit, the ceremony transcript, the
  benchmarks and the example artefacts stay exactly as they are.
- It does not withdraw the ceremony record. The May 2026 ceremony happened and its transcript is
  accurate as a record of it. Its known limitations are disclosed separately.
- It does not claim the original circuit is exploitable in any deployment. It says defects were
  found, and that the repaired circuit is unpublished and unset-up.
